About the Founder

Meet the person behind Quran Atlas and the vision driving the project.

Rao Abdullah

AI Engineer • Software Engineer • Founder of Quran Atlas

Rao Abdullah is an AI Engineer, Software Engineer, and the Founder of Quran Atlas—an AI-powered platform dedicated to transforming how people explore, understand, and engage with the Holy Qur'an through modern technology.

Driven by a passion for combining artificial intelligence with meaningful social impact, he is building Quran Atlas to provide interactive learning experiences, intelligent search, geographical visualization of Qur'anic places, contextual insights, and AI-assisted educational tools that make Qur'anic knowledge more accessible to learners worldwide.

Alongside Quran Atlas, Rao Abdullah focuses on Generative AI, Machine Learning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), backend engineering, and scalable software systems. His mission is to develop intelligent products that solve real-world problems and create lasting educational impact through innovation.

As the founder of Quran Atlas, he is committed to building technology that bridges faith, education, and artificial intelligence, empowering individuals to explore the Qur'an in a more engaging, insightful, and interactive way.
